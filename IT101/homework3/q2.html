Analysis of a Poorly Designed Website

The bad website is Pacific Northwest X-Ray Inc.

10 Reasons Why This Website Has Poor Design

  1. Distracting background: bright blue/turquoise color is very distracting
  2. Poor color contrast: black text blends into certain dark blue parts of the background
  3. Outdated visual style: overall looks super old
  4. Inconsistent graphics: nothing matches and there is no coherence or theme
  5. Repetitive images: skull image repeated doesn't help at all but simply adds visual conflict and confusion
  6. Poor visual hierarchy: doesn't show what is more important
  7. Excessive decoration: super busy and a lot going on in front of viewers' eyes (this makes it hard to focus)
  8. Limited navigation: no main menu button
  9. Unorganized product categories: no grouping of products, they are just listed randomly
  10. Inconsistent spacing and alignment: not structured or organized
